# /trend — Score Trend Tracking

Compare current score against previous scores:
- Show score progression as a timeline
- Category-level deltas (Typography +5, Color -2, etc.)
- Identify sustained improvements vs regressions
- Flag if a category has dropped for 3+ iterations

Useful for tracking design quality over the course of a project.
